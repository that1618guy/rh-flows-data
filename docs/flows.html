Flows that go against the price or against the crowd.

    Who is behind it

    The same flows by wallet size, added across every token.

    How this is measured
    Net flow
    USD bought less USD sold by wallets in each token's main pools, read swap by swap from Robinhood Chain and priced with GeckoTerminal. The share of market cap beside it lets you compare a small token with a large one.
    Wallet size
    By what a wallet held of that token at the start or end of the window, whichever is larger: whales $50K+, big $5K to $50K, mid $500 to $5K, small under $500.
    New wallets
    Held none at the start, net bought, and still hold at least half of it.
    Bots
    Addresses trading more than 100 times a day, and contracts trading 10+ times a day. Counted apart, never inside a wallet group.
    Worth a look
    Whales against the crowd: whales' net is at least 0.05% of market cap and $10K, and smaller wallets took a quarter or more of the other side. Bought on weakness, sold into strength: net flow of 0.1%+ of market cap against a 5%+ price move. Days running: 4+ days in a row. New wallets leading: 15+ new wallets did half or more of the net buying.
    Included
    Crypto tokens on Robinhood Chain with $50K+ of pool liquidity. Left out with 80%+ of volume passed through wallets that held none before and none after: MOW. Stock tokens have their own page.
