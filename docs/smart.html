    The wallets

    Ranked by trading result, Nov 4 to Nov 21. Tap an address to copy it.

    How this is measured
    Who counts
    Up to 100 wallets with the best trading result over every day we have before the latest one (up to 29). Each needs $500+ of result, $5,000+ traded, and 3+ trades on 2+ days. Bots and contracts are left out. The latest day is kept out of the ranking, so what they bought is never the reason they were picked.
    Trading result
    Only on tokens a wallet bought inside the window: what it bought and sold, at the average sell price less the average buy price, plus what it still holds, marked to the last close. Selling old holdings or tokens it was sent never counts as profit.
    The book
    What those wallets still hold of what they bought since the window opened, at the latest close. Positions under $50 aren't counted. On entry is the latest price against the average price they paid.
    Included
    Crypto tokens on Robinhood Chain with $50K+ of pool liquidity. Left out with 80%+ of volume passed through wallets that held none before and none after: MOW. Stock tokens have their own page.
