How to read it
Holders
Wallets holding the token, including smart wallets (EIP-7702 and small wallet proxies). Pools, the Uniswap v4 pool manager, other contracts and burn addresses are left out.
Conviction, 35%
The share of holder supply held for 30+ days by wallets that moved none of it out in that time. 0% scores 0, 80% or more scores 100.
Top holders, 25%
How the 50 largest holders of a week ago changed their combined balance since. Cutting 10% or more scores 0, flat scores 50, adding 10% or more scores 100.
Breadth, 20%
The number of holders worth $10+ against a week ago (at today's price, so price moves don't change the count), averaged with the share of wallets that became holders 8 to 14 days ago and still hold. Shrinking 10% scores 0, growing 10% scores 100; keeping 80% of new holders scores 100.
In profit, 20%
The share of holder supply whose last top-up was at or below today's price: holders sitting on gains have less reason to sell into a bounce. The score is that share.
Fixed scales
Every part uses the same scale for every token and every day, so a 60 means the same thing on PONS as on a meme, and today's score can be compared with last month's.
Tokens
Memes, AI and agent tokens, and ecosystem tokens from Flows. Stock tokens are left out: their holders follow the stock, not the project. Not scored: INU (not enough daily prices).

Data: Robinhood Chain (every transfer since each token's first pool) and GeckoTerminal (prices), through Nov 22, 2026 UTC.
