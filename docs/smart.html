How to read it
Smart money
The 72 wallets with the best trading result from Nov 12 to Nov 20, among wallets that traded $5K+ with 3+ trades on 2+ days and made at least $500. Bots (more than 100 trades a day in a token) and contracts other than smart wallets are left out. The list is picked again every day.
Result
For each token: USD sold less USD bought, plus the tokens the wallet's trades added or removed, valued at the token's close on Nov 20. A wallet that bought below that close or sold above it made money; tokens received or sent by plain transfers never count as profit.
No hindsight
The wallets are picked on the days before Nov 21 and their moves are shown from Nov 21, so what they bought on Nov 21 isn't why they were picked.
Edge
Result divided by USD traded. 10% means they made $10 for every $100 they traded.
Limits
The picking window is short while history fills in (Nov 12 to Nov 20); it grows to 29 days. On days a wallet also moved tokens by plain transfer, its trades are valued at that day's average price, so same-day round trips count less precisely.

Data: Robinhood Chain (swaps, transfers, balances) and GeckoTerminal (prices, pools), through Nov 21, 2026 UTC.
