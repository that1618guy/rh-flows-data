How to read it
Multiple
Fully diluted value divided by a year of revenue at the last 30 days' rate. 5× means the market values the project at five years of its current revenue. Lower is cheaper.
At last week's pace
The same multiple using only the last 7 days of revenue. When it's higher than the multiple, revenue is slowing.
Holder yield
What DefiLlama counts as holder revenue (buybacks, burns and payouts to stakers or holders) over the last 30 days, at a yearly rate, divided by fully diluted value. It's what holders got back, not a promise of what they'll get.
Revenue
The part of fees the project keeps, as DefiLlama defines it for each project (open the project on DefiLlama for its method). Only revenue earned on Robinhood Chain counts.
Flags
Check: below 1×, which usually means the revenue doesn't fully reach the token or the market doubts it will last; read the project's method before calling it cheap. Small float: market cap is under a quarter of fully diluted value, so most of the supply isn't trading yet. Double counted: DefiLlama also counts these fees under another project.
Included
RH-native projects with a token on Robinhood Chain, $50K+ of liquidity and $10K+ of revenue in the last 30 days. Multichain apps are left out.
