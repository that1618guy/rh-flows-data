    The wallets

    How this is measured
    Who counts
    Up to 100 wallets with the best trading result over the days before the window you pick, as far back as we hold (30 days in all). Each needs $500+ of result, $5,000+ traded, and 3+ trades on 2+ days. Bots and contracts are left out. The window itself is kept out of the ranking, so what they bought is never the reason they were picked.
    1D and 7D
    What those wallets did on the latest day, or over the latest 7. A 7-day window leaves fewer days to rank on, so its list of wallets is a little different. With everyone else's figure it adds up to the Flows page's net for the same days.
    Trading result
    Only on tokens a wallet bought inside the window: what it bought and sold, at the average sell price less the average buy price, plus what it still holds, marked to the last close. Selling old holdings or tokens it was sent never counts as profit.
    The book
    What those wallets still hold of what they bought since the ranking days opened, at the latest close. Positions under $50 aren't counted. On entry is the latest price against the average price they paid.
    Included
    Crypto tokens on Robinhood Chain with $50K+ of pool liquidity. Left out with 80%+ of volume passed through wallets that held none before and none after: MOW. Stock tokens have their own page.
