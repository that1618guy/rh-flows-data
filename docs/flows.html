Who's buying Robinhood Chain

Through Nov 21, 2026, end of day UTC. Read from Robinhood Chain, swap by swap, and priced with GeckoTerminal. Describes on-chain behaviour; not investment advice.

Across the chain

Net: each group's net in every token shown, added together. All wallets is the four size groups together; fresh wallets and bots are counted apart from it.

Stock tokens: minted vs bought

Minted is the change in each stock token's supply on the chain. DEX net is what wallets bought less what they sold in the pools; bots are the high-frequency traders and arbitrage contracts, counted apart.

How to read it
Net
USD bought less USD sold in each token's main pools (pools with $5K+ of liquidity on Uniswap v3 and v4, Aerodrome Slipstream, Ramses and other v2/v3-style DEXs). Every swap is read from Robinhood Chain and valued at that hour's price.
Who traded
The wallet whose token balance changed in the swap. Routers and aggregators pass tokens through and net to zero, so the trade lands on the wallet behind it.
Wallet size
Each wallet that traded is sorted by its holding at the window's start or end, whichever is larger: whales $50K+, big $5K to $50K, mid $500 to $5K, small under $500. A whale that sold everything still counts as a whale.
Fresh wallets
Wallets that held none of the token at the start of the window, ended up net buyers, and still hold at least half of what they bought. It's the closest on-chain read of new money that stayed.
Pass-through
The share of a token's volume from wallets that held none of it before and none after: they bought and moved it on, or received and sold it. A high share usually means volume farming or wash trading.
Bots and contracts
Wallets trading more than 100 times a day, and arbitrage, market-making and vault contracts trading 10+ times a day. They're shown apart and never inside a size group. Smart wallets (most RH traders use EIP-7702 or small wallet proxies) count as wallets.
Checks
What wallets traded matches GeckoTerminal's volume for the same pools within about 7% for memes (CASHCAT +6%, AI −7% on Nov 20). Stock tokens read lower (SPCX −14%) because arbitrage that buys in one pool and sells in another inside a single transaction nets to zero for the wallet, while GeckoTerminal counts both legs.
Windows
1D is the latest full UTC day, 7D the latest seven. 30D appears once 30 days are recorded.
Not counted
Plain transfers between wallets, adding or removing liquidity, and pools under $5K. Stock tokens' minting is shown separately, never as buying.
Tokens
32 tokens: memes and AI tokens with $100K+ of liquidity and 14+ days of trading, the largest stock tokens, and ecosystem tokens. The list is reviewed by hand. HOOD is left out: it copies the stock ticker but isn't a stock token. Not shown today: INU (no price data).

Data: Robinhood Chain (swaps, transfers, balances) and GeckoTerminal (prices, pools), through Nov 21, 2026 UTC.
