Smart Filter · Nov 25 · updated daily

One token to watch.

RAETO for an exit. Every flag is scored on what price did after.

On trial

Filters that are logged and scored the same way but never posted. One moves up when its record earns it.

How this is measured
What it does
It runs every token through a few filters on what its wallets are doing and lists the ones that pass. Each token gets one line a day at most, the same story isn't repeated on the days after, and a day lists 4 at most. On some days nothing passes.
Exit
Large wallets (holding $5K+ of the token) net sold a big amount over 7 days, and the 50 largest holders of a week ago cut their combined balance by 8% or more. A big amount is 0.5% of market cap, never under $20K or over $100K; an exit needs 1.5 of them.
Smart money leaving
The most profitable wallets, ranked only on the days before, net sold $10K+ on the day, with at least 3 of them selling and at least 2 more selling than buying.
Washout
Price down 20%+ on the day. The ticket says what large wallets and new wallets did into it, and whether the 50 largest holders have been cutting.
What happened after
The token's close on the day it was flagged against its close 7 days later (3 days for a washout). The median token is the middle result of every token on these pages over the same days, so a flag is judged against the pack, not against zero.
The record
It starts on Oct 22 and is still short, so read every figure as early. None of these is a proven edge.
Included
Crypto tokens on Robinhood Chain with $50K+ of pool liquidity. Left out with 80%+ of volume passed through wallets that held none before and none after: MOW. Data, not advice.
