Holder strength, 0 to 100

    How this is measured
    1D, 7D, 30D
    The window sets what today's score is compared with: a day, a week or 30 days ago. The score itself is the same in all three, and its parts keep their own spans (top holders over 7 days, conviction over 30). A token gets a 30-day comparison once it has 60 days of prices.
    Who is a holder
    Wallets only. Pools, other contracts and burn addresses are left out. Balances are rebuilt from every transfer since the token's first pool and match its total supply.
    Conviction (35%)
    Share of holder supply held 30+ days by wallets that moved none out in that time. 0% scores 0, 80% scores 100.
    Top holders (25%)
    How the 50 largest holders of a week ago changed their combined balance. −10% scores 0, flat scores 50, +10% scores 100.
    Breadth (20%)
    Holders worth $10+ against a week ago, averaged with the share of last week's new holders who are still in.
    In profit (20%)
    Share of holder supply whose last top-up was at or below today's price.
    Where holders stand
    Each holder's entry is the price on the day they last added. Supply is split by how far today's price is from that entry; the average holder's result is today's price against the supply-weighted average entry. Not part of the score.
    Included
    Crypto tokens on Robinhood Chain with $50K+ of pool liquidity and 30+ days of daily prices. Left out today (80%+ of volume passed through wallets that held none before and none after: MOW; too few daily prices: TEXEL).
